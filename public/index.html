Wonder Wall

Play together, without talking. Tap a collectible sticker at a station and your character joins a shared world on the wall. Every tap from anyone unlocks more for everyone.

🖼️
WallOpen on the big screen or projector. Press F11 for full screen.
📲
StationOpen on a phone or tablet next to the wall. It reads stickers by NFC or QR.

What’s real and what’s simulated

PieceIn this prototype
Shared world synced across devicesReal Next.js API on Vercel. State is kept in Redis (Upstash), and walls check for updates about every second.
NFC sticker tapReal on Chrome for Android (Web NFC). Works with written or blank NTAG stickers.
QR on the stickerReal station camera scan, or any phone’s camera app.
Projected wallStand-in a laptop or TV in a browser. A projector plugs into the same page.
Dedicated reader kioskStand-in a phone or tablet running the Station page.
“Simulate a sticker tap” buttonsSimulated for demos with no sticker. The wall feed marks these taps.
